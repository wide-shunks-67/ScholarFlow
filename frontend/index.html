Audience & Fit

Who ScholarFlow is built for

Realistic, targeted assistance for undergraduate and postgraduate students reaching out to academic research labs.

Internship Applicants

Students preparing for winter or summer research internships who need an organized way to find relevant faculty by topic rather than scrolling through endless university department directories.

First-Time Cold Outreach

Students wanting to avoid generic flattery or overly long emails. ScholarFlow provides concise (<180 words) structures that highlight reproducible work and concrete contributions.

Methodical Planners

Students who want to prepare code baselines over a 6-week schedule, record sent inquiries, and manage a respectful 7-day follow-up cadence without spreadsheet chaos.

Honest expectations: ScholarFlow provides tools to structure your search and compose polite emails. Response rates depend entirely on faculty capacity, funding, and your technical match; using ScholarFlow does not guarantee an interview, response, or internship offer.

The Research Outreach Workflow

Four steps from cold search to confirmed stay

Academic outreach fails when emails are generic, unscheduled, and untracked. ScholarFlow structures the entire lifecycle into an organized pipeline.

Step 01

Find Research Mentors

Search over 7,000 faculty records across 71 premier institutes by department, lab topics, or specific keywords like Graph Neural Networks or Microfluidics.

Step 02

Draft Targeted Outreach

Generate concise outreach drafts based on your entered project details and the professor's listed research area. Includes heuristic keyword-alignment feedback.

Step 03

Follow 6-Week Roadmap

A systematic guide through domain selection, baseline reproduction, GitHub report preparation, cold outreach waves, and interview practice.

Step 04

Track Applications

Log contact dates, track statuses (Applied, Follow-up, Interview, Accepted), monitor 7-day follow-up cadences, and export data as CSV anytime.

How ScholarFlow Handles Your Data

Transparent privacy details based on the exact codebase implementation.

Local Browser Storage

Your student profile, roadmap progress, and outreach tracker entries are saved exclusively in your browser’s localStorage. No application data is sent to external cloud databases.

Bundled Faculty Dataset

The faculty directory contains 7,055 records compiled from institutional faculty listings across 71 premier Indian engineering institutions. Search and filtering execute 100% in-browser in JavaScript memory.

Direct Mailto Launching

Emails are drafted locally and launched directly via your system’s default mail handler (mailto:) or copied to clipboard. ScholarFlow never sends emails on your behalf.

Native Web Speech Audio

The 30-second elevator pitch feature uses your browser's native Web Speech Synthesis API. No audio recordings or transcripts are transmitted over the network.
